Text overlays appear everywhere in our daily video streaming experiences. Where should we place the text, and how should the text look like?

I designed, programmed, and conducted an end-to-end user study about how subtitle design affects viewing
experience in high-dynamic-range (HDR) video (paper in prep!).

For this study, I built a video-stimulus generation pipeline using speaker detection and image analysis to systematically
manipulate viewing conditions in HDR content.

With the results from the study, I optimized text placement in videos, maximizing readability, comfort, and engagement.
