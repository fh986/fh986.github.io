Check out my VSS 2025 poster [here](/assets/pdf/Bruns_VSS25_poster.pdf)!

Motivation: We need to effectively correct for noise in psychological measurements to ensure robust insights.
Project outcome: developed a framework and built a user-friendly toolbox for noise correction. Second-author poster presented at VSS 2025. Co-first author manuscript in prep.
Project details: The toolbox helps researchers plan experiments based on the characteristics of the variables being studied (e.g., measurement noise) and practical constraints (e.g., recruitment budget). It recommends experiment designs and visualizes the expected data.
Skills: Statistical modeling & simulation | Quantitative research methods | Data visualization | User-centered design
