Research question: Do visual cues like a speaker’s face improve word recognition, and do they increase listening effort or fatigue?

Project outcome: identified the most appropriate method for testing listening effort. Found that the natural face improves word recognition and decreases listening effort. Poster presented at the Psychonomics Society conference, 2022.

Project details: compared different measures of listening effort and implemented a carefully -designed survey. With 100+ participants, compared word recognition and listening effort with 4 different visual cues.

Skills: 
- A/B test
- Survey
- Audio stimuli encoding
- Human behavioral testing
