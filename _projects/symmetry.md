Check out the published paper [here](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0042698924000993)!

Research question: How does spacing between visual elements affect symmetry perception?

Project outcome: Found that people detect symmetry less accurately as spacing increases, revealing limits of visual integration. First author paper published in Vision Research. 

Project details: Design and ran online experiments in 40+ participants. Used patterns of varying separations to test visual processing efficiency.
