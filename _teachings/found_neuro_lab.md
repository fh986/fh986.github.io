## Course Overview

This course is an introduction to basic neural function. Topics include neural transmission, development of the nervous system, anatomy, sensory systems, learning and the corresponding change in the brain, and the role of the nervous system in behavior. The lab portion of the course involves experiments and projects about recording neural activities, brain dissections, imaging methods, developing and implementing animal behavioral experiments.

## My responsibilites

As the teaching assistant, I assisted in designing, developing, and implementing the course and the lab. I also advised students during office hours, going over course materials and teaching scientific writing. For the lab, I set up neuroscience/behavioral testing rigs, set up and helped teach sheep brain dissections, and helped students to develop end-to-end neuroscience projects.
