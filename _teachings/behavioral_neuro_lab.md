## Course Overview

This course covers the physiological bases of complex behaviors in mammals, with an emphasis on neural and hormonal mechanisms. The lab portion of the course includes the effects of anxiogenic drugs on rat's anxiety levels, brain dissection and anatomy, and clinical psychology practices.

## My responsibilites

As the teaching assistant, I assisted in the development and implementation of the lab course, including preparing for and assisting during rat surgeries, setting up behavioral testing rigs, and preparing other materials and apparatus for the lab course.
